# Website content

- Preserve the user's existing wording unless they explicitly request a rewrite.
- Never use em dashes or en dashes in website copy, UI messages, metadata, SEO text, or structured data. Use a period, comma, colon, parentheses, or a plain hyphen as appropriate.
- Keep the certification label as "iSAQB CPSA-F" and its expanded title as "Certified Professional for Software Architecture". Do not append "Foundation Level" in website content, including CV pages and SEO. Keep the hyphen in "CPSA-F".
- Keep edits scoped to the requested content or layout. Do not change global page widths or typography for a local card adjustment.

# Commit messages

- Conventional Commits are mandatory for every commit, including amended commits.
- Use `<type>[optional scope]: <description>`, for example `feat(portfolio): add certification` or `docs: require conventional commits`.
- Choose the type that matches the change, such as `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, `style`, `test`, `build`, `ci`, `perf`, or `chore`.
